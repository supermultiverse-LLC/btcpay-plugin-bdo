# **The Taproot Assets Infrastructure Manifesto**

**For anyone who believes digital ownership should be as sovereign as money.**

---

## **The Belief**

Bitcoin gave us digital money without intermediaries.

We believe ownership deserves the same freedom.

**Digital ownership should be as sovereign as money itself.**

Not through custodians. Not through bridges. Not through sidechains.

But through the same principle that made Bitcoin valuable: you can verify everything yourself, without permission, without intermediaries.

---

## **Why This Moment**

**Bitcoin solved money.** It proved digital value could exist without a trusted third party.

**Lightning solved payments.** It proved Bitcoin could scale to real-world transaction volume.

**Taproot Assets solve ownership.** It proves Bitcoin can transport verifiable digital property.

But Bitcoin didn't change the world with code alone.

It changed the world when infrastructure existed. When operators could run full nodes. When mining pools formed. When exchanges integrated it.

Technology without infrastructure remains a laboratory.

Technology with infrastructure becomes an economy.

We are building that infrastructure for digital ownership on Bitcoin.

---

## **The Problem We're Solving**

Digital asset infrastructure has become synonymous with giving up control.

Today you choose between:

* **Custodians** who hold your keys  
* **Bridges** that wrap your assets in additional trust assumptions  
* **Sidechains** that leave Bitcoin's security behind  
* **Proprietary platforms** that lock you into their ecosystem

This is broken.

**We choose differently.**

We choose Bitcoin-native solutions whenever they exist. We choose infrastructure that puts you in control. We choose professional tools that work for operators, not gatekeepers.

The technology exists. Taproot Assets are real. They live on Bitcoin. Proofs are verifiable on-chain.

But there is no easy way for professionals to operate them.

No standard. No open infrastructure.

**This is what we are building.**

---

This is what happens when infrastructure is built on principles instead of convenience.

## **The Core Principles**

These are non-negotiable. They define what we are.

**1\. Open Source Without Asterisks**

Apache 2.0. Always.

Not "open source with proprietary backend."

Not "open source but our cloud service is required."

The entire stack is yours to audit, fork, modify, and run.

This is the foundation of trust.

**2\. Bitcoin Native, Always**

Digital ownership lives on Bitcoin. Not on sidechains. Not on token layers. Not on bridges.

Proofs are anchored to Bitcoin. Verification is Bitcoin-native.

Every design decision starts here.

**3\. No Proprietary Dependencies**

If Supermultiverse disappeared tomorrow, this infrastructure still works.

That's not a bug. That's the design.

Operators control their destiny, not us.

**4\. Minimize Trust, Never Maximize It**

Every dependency is a trust assumption.

We eliminate them aggressively.

Your keys are your keys. Your nodes are your nodes.

Bitcoin is the only oracle.

**5\. Built for Professionals and Developers**

We build for people who understand infrastructure.

Operators, enterprises, developers, builders.

Not for casual users seeking "easy solutions."

If you can run Bitcoin Core, you can run this.

And if you're a developer, this is something you can build on.

**6\. Standards Over Innovation**

We implement open standards:

* Taproot Assets specification (Lightning Labs)  
* BTCPay plugin architecture (BTCPay Server)  
* REST APIs (tapd standard)  
* Bitcoin and Lightning (the foundation)

We don't invent proprietary layers. We implement what works.

## **The Precedent**

BTCPay Server proved something remarkable.

It took Lightning — complex, technical, confusing — and made it accessible to merchants who just wanted to accept Bitcoin without intermediaries.

You could run your own node. You could hold your keys. You could operate professionally without dependency on Stripe or PayPal.

Lightning didn't change the world with the protocol.

It changed the world when professionals could use it.

**We are doing the same thing for digital ownership.**

Just as BTCPay democratized Lightning for operators, this infrastructure democratizes Taproot Assets for anyone who wants to custody property on Bitcoin.

The technology existed in both cases.

The missing piece was professional infrastructure.

We are building that missing piece.

**Proprietary Backend**

No SaaS to "simplify" asset management.

No "use our relay" to skip complexity.

Operators control their infrastructure or it's not a solution.

**Sacrifice Open Source**

Not for speed. Not for deadlines. Not for convenience.

Slower with integrity beats faster without it.

**Optimize for One Issuer**

Supermultiverse is the first user, not the only user.

Decisions are made for the ecosystem, not for SMV.

**Create Lock-In**

No "you can only withdraw to X wallet."

No "this only works with our API."

Operators must have portability or we failed.

**Pretend to Be Consumer Software**

This is not "easy Bitcoin assets for everyone."

If you want that, use Supermultiverse Wallet.

This is professional infrastructure.

Own it fully or it's not right for you.
